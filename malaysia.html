Overview

Malaysia has emerged as a popular education destination for international students, offering quality education at affordable costs. With a multicultural environment and English-medium instruction, Malaysia provides an excellent alternative to Western education.

Why Study in Malaysia?

  • Affordable tuition fees compared to UK/USA/Australia
  • English-language programmes available
  • Close proximity to home for Asian students
  • Multicultural society (Malay, Chinese, Indian communities)
  • Modern infrastructure and facilities
  • Partnerships with UK/Australian universities (twinned programmes)
  • Lower cost of living

Many Malaysian universities offer twinned degree programmes where you can study partial years in Malaysia and complete in the UK/Australia at reduced costs.

Cost of Living

International students should budget approximately RM1,500-2,500 per month for living expenses, including accommodation, food, transport, and personal costs.

Intakes

Malaysian universities typically offer three intakes: January, May/June, and September/October.

Enrollment Process

The enrollment process for Malaysian universities is straightforward and less competitive than UK universities.

Step-by-Step Guide

  • Research: Explore universities and courses
  • Check Requirements: Academic and English requirements
  • Prepare Documents: Transcripts, passport, photos
  • Submit Application: Direct to university or through agent
  • Receive Offer: Offer letter within 2-4 weeks
  • Accept Offer: Pay tuition fee deposit
  • Apply for Visa: Submit visa application

Apply at least 3-6 months before your intended intake for smooth processing.

Required Documents

  • Valid Passport
  • Academic Transcripts
  • English Proficiency Certificate (IELTS 5.0-5.5)
  • Passport-sized Photos
  • Application Form
  • Proof of Funds

Guidelines

Before applying to Malaysian universities, ensure you meet the following requirements.

Academic Requirements

  • Undergraduate: SPM/O-levels, A-levels, or equivalent with 60%+
  • Postgraduate: Bachelor's degree with 2.5+ CGPA
  • Foundation: For students not meeting direct entry

English Language Requirements

  • IELTS: 5.0-5.5 minimum
  • TOEFL iBT: 60-80
  • MUET: Band 3-4 (for local universities)
  • Some universities offer conditional admission without English scores

Check specific course requirements as they vary by university and programme. Some courses may have additional requirements.

Financial Requirements

Show proof of funds for tuition and living expenses. Bank statements for the last 3 months are typically required.

Visa Application

International students need a Student Pass to study in Malaysia. The process is managed by the Immigration Department.

Student Pass Requirements

  • Offer Letter from University
  • Valid Passport (minimum 12 months)
  • Proof of Finances
  • Passport-sized Photos
  • Travel Insurance
  • Completed Application Form

Your university will typically assist with the visa application process. Processing takes 2-6 weeks.

Visa Fees

  • Application Fee: RM (varies by university)
  • Student Pass: RM (depending on course duration)
  • Processing fee: As per IMF guidelines

After Arrival

Upon arrival, you must register with the immigration department and collect your Student Pass card from the university.
